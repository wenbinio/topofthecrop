# Checking the app works

A walk through every screen with what you should see. The figures are the demo field's, from the data of 14 November 2026; if the data is replaced, they change.

Open https://wenbinio.github.io/topofthecrop/ and press **Ctrl+F5** first, so the browser fetches the newest version. Shortcut to the demo field: https://wenbinio.github.io/topofthecrop/#/questions?f=genzano-demo

## Signs something is broken, on any screen

- A blank page, or "Loading…" for more than 10 seconds.
- A 0, "NaN" or "undefined" where a figure should be, or a code such as `year.rainHead` instead of words.
- English words in Italian (dataset names and "about 11 km" are still English: known).
- Text or a table cut off at the right edge of a phone.
- A figure's **Source** (or the figure itself, on Options and Year) should open a small bubble with its dataset, date and confidence; a tap anywhere else should close it.
- **Not a bug:** "not available" under *This season so far* or *Next 7 days* means NASA POWER or MET Norway did not answer. Tap *Try again*.

## 1. Find the field

- [ ] The map opens on the Bradano basin; the white line is the basin edge.
- [ ] Tap the field near Genzano di Lucania: "about 6 ha · Is this your field?" Tap **Yes**.

## 2. Questions

- [ ] *Last crop* is already **Durum** (EU crop map 2022).
- [ ] Tap **Income**, then **Water**: they get the numbers 1 and 2.

## 3. Options (Income first, Water second)

- [ ] *What we see*: bare after harvest **2 months**, hot flowering **4 days**, autumn rain **156 mm**.
- [ ] Order: **Chickpea 729 €/ha/yr** (Best fit), Lentil 597, Coriander 548, Rapeseed 621.

## 4. Why (tap Chickpea)

- [ ] The screen opens with *Why suggested*, six steps: less green than usual; more hot days at flowering; decline with heat; area-wide; chose income; chose water.
- [ ] *Over three harvests*: per year **729**; durum 2028 shows "+16% grain after chickpea", 2029 "+10%". *What the columns mean* opens on a tap.
- [ ] *Evidence*, each line once, each with a *Source* to tap: hot flowering days **4** (range 0–8), bare months **2** (1–6), Oct–May rain **542 mm** (418–631), water durum needs **509 mm** (487–544), topsoil clay **37%**, soil water in spring **30.1%** (27.4–32), water crops used **319 mm** (277–346).

## 5. Year

- [ ] **Normal:** hot days **4 → 5.4** by 2050; rain **542 → 520 mm**; "A usual season (2001–2020)."
- [ ] Slide to **Drier:** **8 → 10** days, **418 → 393 mm**. **Much drier:** **13 → 15** days, **390 → 367 mm**.
- [ ] *This season so far* (live): rain since 1 September, usual by now, a verdict, the last day with 5 mm or more, and "Not counted: NASA POWER rain for October 2025…".
- [ ] *Next 7 days* (live): rain for each day and the coldest night.
- [ ] *2024 and 2026 against a usual season*: 2024 **413 mm ↓, 27.3% ↓, 0.60, 258 mm ↓**; 2026 **651 mm ↑, 32.8% ↑, 0.72, 326 mm**; then "Much less green than nearby fields in 2018 and 2023…". Nothing cut off on a phone.

## 6. Check and share

- [ ] What to check yourself, who else can check, then Print, WhatsApp and Copy link.
- [ ] *Data and credits* at the bottom opens a page of its own with the credits and the AI note; *Back* returns.

## 7. Prices and support (top bar)

- [ ] Change the durum price: Chickpea's income changes and shows **Yours**.
- [ ] Copy the link and open it in a new tab: the change is still there.

## 8. Your own field

- [ ] Draw a field **inside** the white line: figures say **Area** (the 1 km around your field) and differ from the demo's.
- [ ] Draw one **outside**: *Area weather* shows hot days, autumn rain and late frost from NASA POWER, with "Not counted: NASA POWER rain for October 2025…".
