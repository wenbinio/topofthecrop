# Checking the app works

A walk through every screen with what you should see. The figures are the demo field's, from the data of 14 November 2026; if the data is replaced, they change.

Open https://wenbinio.github.io/topofthecrop/ and press **Ctrl+F5** first, so the browser fetches the newest version. Shortcut to the demo field: https://wenbinio.github.io/topofthecrop/#/questions?f=genzano-demo

## Signs something is broken, on any screen

- A blank page, or "Loading…" for more than 10 seconds.
- A 0, "NaN" or "undefined" where a figure should be, or a code such as `year.rainHead` instead of words.
- English words in Italian (dataset names and "about 11 km" are still English: known).
- Text or a table cut off at the right edge of a phone.
- A figure's **Source** (or the figure itself, on Options and Year) should open a small bubble with its dataset, date and confidence; a tap anywhere else should close it.
- **Not a bug:** "not available" under *This season so far* or *Next 7 days* means NASA POWER or MET Norway did not answer. Tap *Try again*.

## 1. Find the field

- [ ] The map opens on the Bradano basin; the white line is the basin edge.
- [ ] Tap the field near Genzano di Lucania: "about 6 ha · Is this your field?" Tap **Yes**.

## 2. Questions

- [ ] *Last crop* is already **Durum** (EU crop map 2022).
- [ ] Tap **Income**, then **Water**: they get the numbers 1 and 2.

## 3. Options (Income first, Water second)

- [ ] *What we see*: bare after harvest **2 months**, hot flowering **4 days**, autumn rain **156 mm**.
- [ ] Order: **Chickpea 715 €/ha/yr** (Best fit), Lentil 603, Coriander 555, Rapeseed 628.

## 4. Why (tap Chickpea)

- [ ] The screen opens with *Why this option*, six steps: less green than usual; more hot flowering days; falling off with heat; across the area; picked income; picked water.
- [ ] *Over three harvests*: per year **715**; durum 2028 shows "+16% grain after chickpea", 2029 "+10%". *What the columns mean* opens on a tap.
- [ ] *What the data shows*, each line once, each with a *Source* to tap: hot flowering days **4** (range 0–8), bare months **2** (1–6), Oct–May rain **542 mm** (418–631), water durum needs **509 mm** (487–544), topsoil clay **37%**, soil water in spring **30.1%** (27.4–32), water crops used **319 mm** (277–346).

## 5. Year

- [ ] Three cards, no slider. **Usual year:** hot days **4** now, **5.4** by 2050; rain **542** and **520 mm**; "A usual season (2001–2020)."
- [ ] **Dry year:** **8** and **10** days, **418** and **393 mm**. **Driest year:** **13** and **15** days, **390** and **367 mm**. Each card ends with its best fit.
- [ ] *This season so far* (live): rain since 1 September, usual by now, a verdict, the last day with 5 mm or more, and "We left out NASA POWER rain for October 2025…".
- [ ] *Next 7 days* (live): rain for each day and the coldest night.
- [ ] *Your field from space* (near the top, under the heading) opens a timelapse: dated pictures with the field's yellow outline filling the frame, play, pause and a slider. *Back* returns.
- [ ] *2024 and 2026 compared with a usual season*: 2024 **413 mm ↓, 27.3% ↓, 0.60, 258 mm ↓**; 2026 **651 mm ↑, 32.8% ↑, 0.72, 326 mm**; then "Much less green than nearby fields in 2018 and 2023…". Nothing cut off on a phone.

- [ ] *Print page* ends with the same credits.
- [ ] *Print page*, before the credits: *What the data shows* has bars for 2024 and 2026 against a usual season (rain **542, 413, 651 mm**; soil water **30.1, 27.3, 32.8%**; greenness **0.71, 0.60, 0.72**; water used **319, 258, 326 mm**), hot flowering days **4** today and **5.4** and **6** by 2041–2060 with a line on each future bar, and the rain check (station **674** and **315 mm**, satellites **498** and **308 mm**). Then *How the seasons went*: four line graphs, the last five seasons darker.

## 7. Prices and support (top bar)

- [ ] Change the durum price: Chickpea's income changes and shows **Yours**.
- [ ] Copy the link and open it in a new tab: the change is still there.

## 8. Your own field

- [ ] Draw a field **inside** the white line: figures say **Area** (the 1 km around your field) and differ from the demo's.
- [ ] Draw one **outside**: *Area weather* shows hot days, autumn rain and late frost from NASA POWER, with "We left out NASA POWER rain for October 2025…".

- [ ] Tap **it**: every screen in Italian, apart from dataset names.

## Reporting a problem

Send a screenshot, the step number above, and the page's link (copy it from the address bar: it holds every choice made).

## 11. One thing at a time

- [ ] Questions: "Question 1 of 3" (last crop), Next, "Question 2 of 3" (limits), Next, "Question 3 of 3" (priorities). Next waits only on the last, for two priorities.
- [ ] Options: the advice and its two reasons, then six rows under *Tap to look closer*. Each opens smoothly on a tap and closes on the next; nothing jumps.
- [ ] Why: the reasons are open; *Over three harvests*, *What the data shows*, *Benefits*, *What we don't know* and *Check locally* open on a tap.
- [ ] The switch under the other options is called **Intercropping**, and its knob slides.
